# S11-T01 — Research conservative visual parser

**Status:** COMPLETED. **Branch:** `codex/sprint-11-visual-editor` tracking `origin/codex/sprint-11-visual-editor`. **Base:** `codex/sprint-10-academic-editing`. **Environment:** Linux (x86_64), Node 26+, pnpm 11.25.0. **Actual effort:** 4 h.

## Scope and acceptance evidence

- Conducted 4h research spike evaluating conservative LaTeX visual parsing, AST representations, range tracking fidelity, and licensing.
- Evaluated `@unified-latex/unified-latex` (MIT license) versus specialized zero-copy range-mapped projection:
  - AST re-serialization in general parsers causes non-localized formatting churn and comment/whitespace loss.
  - Selected conservative source AST projection with exact character ranges `[start, end]` and SHA-256 fragment hashes.
- Established matrix of supported visual nodes vs literal fallback nodes according to Section 10 of Master Plan and EDT-13:
  - Supported: Headings (`\section`, `\subsection`, `\subsubsection`), paragraphs, bold (`\textbf`), italic (`\textit`, `\emph`), lists (`itemize`, `enumerate`), math (inline `$ ... $`, display `\[ ... \]`, `\begin{equation}`), figures (`figure`, `\includegraphics`), tables (`tabular`), citations (`\cite`), references (`\ref`, `\eqref`).
  - Literal fallback: Preamble (`\documentclass`, `\usepackage`), unknown macros, macro definitions (`\newcommand`, `\def`), `verbatim`, TikZ (`\begin{tikzpicture}`).
- Defined minimal splice patching protocol (`VisualPatch`) with fragment hash optimistic concurrency guard preventing overwrite of concurrent changes.
- Documented findings, architectural decisions, and boundaries in `docs/adr/008-visual-parser.md`.
